## Problem Title
SIH 25010: Smart Crop Advisory System for Small and Marginal Farmers
## Problem Description
A majority of small and marginal farmers in India rely on traditional knowledge, local shopkeepers, or guesswork for crop selection, pest control, and fertilizer use. They lack access to personalized, real-time advisory services that account for soil type, weather conditions, and crop history. This often leads to poor yield, excessive input costs, and environmental degradation due to overuse of chemicals. Language barriers, low digital literacy, and absence of localized tools further limit their access to modern agri-tech resources.

Impact / Why this problem needs to be solved

Helping small farmers make informed decisions can significantly increase productivity, reduce costs, and improve livelihoods. It also contributes to sustainable farming practices, food security, and environmental conservation. A smart advisory solution can empower farmers with scientific insights in their native language and reduce dependency on unreliable third-party advice.

Expected Outcomes

• A multilingual, AI-based mobile app or chatbot that provides real-time, location-specific crop advisory. • Soil health recommendations and fertilizer guidance. • Weather-based alerts and predictive insights. • Pest/disease detection via image uploads. • Market price tracking. • Voice support for low-literate users. • Feedback and usage data collection for continuous improvement.

Relevant Stakeholders / Beneficiaries

• Small and marginal farmers • Agricultural extension officers • Government agriculture departments • NGOs and cooperatives • Agri-tech startups

Supporting Data

• 86% of Indian farmers are small or marginal (NABARD Report, 2022). • Studies show ICT-based advisories can increase crop yield by 20–30%.

## Proposed Solution
The solution is a multilingual, AI-powered mobile and voice-enabled advisory platform designed specifically for small and marginal farmers. The platform delivers personalized, real-time crop, soil, weather, pest, and market advice, leveraging advanced AI and easy digital interfaces. Key features include:

Crop Selection Guidance: Utilizes localized data (soil, weather, crop history) for recommending optimal crops.

Soil Health & Fertilizer Advice: Integrates soil test results or predictive modeling for tailored fertilizer recommendations, reducing overuse.

Weather Alerts: Automated forecasting and risk alerts for timely action (irrigation, protection measures).

Pest/Disease Detection: AI image analysis enables farmers to upload pictures of suspicious crop symptoms for instant diagnosis and treatment suggestions.

Market Price Tracking: Real-time mandi prices help farmers make informed sales and maximize profits.

Voice and Multilingual Support: Native language options and audio input/output for low-literacy users.

Feedback Mechanism: Collects on-ground farmer responses to continually improve algorithms.

This comprehensive package helps farmers move away from guesswork and third-party dependency, directly addressing issues of poor yield, high input costs, and environmental damage from excessive chemical use.

## Technical Approach
Technologies Used Programming Languages: Python (AI/ML backend), Java/Kotlin (Android app), React Native (cross-platform UI).

Frameworks: TensorFlow/Keras for machine learning, Django/Flask for backend, OpenWeatherMap and mandi APIs for data feeds.

Hardware (Optional): Smartphone, feature phones (for SMS/Voice); scalable to IoT sensors/drones in future.

Cloud & Edge AI: Azure/AWS/Google Cloud for scalable data processing, with edge AI for offline diagnostics.
